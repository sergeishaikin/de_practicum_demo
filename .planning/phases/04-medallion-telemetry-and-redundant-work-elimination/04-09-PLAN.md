<objective>
Measure what the phase actually bought, on a bounded workload, and record it in a form
that cannot be mistaken for more than it is.

Purpose: BENCH-01 is the phase's honesty gate. Every earlier plan claims a saving from a
five-cycle demo-volume baseline; this plan produces the post-change measurement and
states precisely how comparable the two are.

Output: three evidence artifacts under `artifacts/phase-04/`, following the shape
established by `artifacts/b2-rollout/06-o1-window.json`, `06-o1-summary.json` and
`06-bounded-workload.json`.

**This plan is deliberately not autonomous.** It requires a live stack and a deliberate,
authorised state mutation, which `AGENTS.md` and `CLAUDE.md` both place outside
read-only work. Task 1 is a blocking authorisation gate and nothing after it may run
until the operator answers.

**Scope boundary on "the canonical warehouse must be untouched".** Running the medallion
against the canonical `dwh` with `METRICS_ENABLED=1` writes to
`marts.lakehouse_metrics` by definition — that is the measurement. So the requirement is
stated exactly: `marts.lakehouse_metrics` is the **only** canonical relation permitted to
change, every other relation must be proved unchanged by content fingerprint, and nothing
may run at all without the operator's explicit authorisation.
</objective>
