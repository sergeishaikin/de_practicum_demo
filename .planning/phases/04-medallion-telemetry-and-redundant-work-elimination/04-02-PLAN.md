<objective>
Give `marts.lakehouse_metrics` a cycle and phase identity, stop the Prometheus
gauge-reset defect, and make the historical interpretation rule executable.

Purpose: MTL-01 is unachievable while the sink cannot express "which cycle, which
phase". Separately, `_RuntimeMetrics` labels every gauge by `source` alone, so the outer
medallion record currently overwrites the nested B2 gauges **with zeros** —
`lakehouse_files{kind="planned"}`, `lakehouse_bytes`, `lakehouse_processed{kind="keys"}`
and `lakehouse_work{state="in_flight"}` reset seconds after being measured, which
directly weakens the `LakehouseUnresolvedWork` alert. That is an active defect and its
fix is in scope. MTL-02 turns the corrected, status-qualified reading of pre-Phase-4
rows into a tested function rather than a paragraph.

Output: seven additive nullable/defaulted columns, seven new keyword-only
`Metrics.record` parameters, cycle-only Prometheus observation, and
`classify_metric_row`.

**Locked constraint:** do **not** add a `phase` label to any Prometheus collector. The
operator's locked decision is cycle-only gauge updates: only the outer cycle record
touches gauges; nested phase records go to PostgreSQL only. No new label, no cardinality
change, no dashboard or alert-rule rewrite.
</objective>
